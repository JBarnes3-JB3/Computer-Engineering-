For the 8-puzzle with both single and double moves, I designed an admissible heuristic h3. This guesses the least number of moves needed to reach the target state by using the Manhattan distance, considering that it's possible to make double moves. This shows that a tile can shift two places in one move through a double move. The method assures an underestimated actual cost calculation, as no tile can reach its destination in fewer than the calculated moves. This means the heuristic does not overestimate the best path ever. h3 provides a steady, computationally efficient, and permissible estimate that successfully directs the A* algorithm to solve the 8-puzzle optimally. It utilizes double moves advantage without sacrificing accuracy.
